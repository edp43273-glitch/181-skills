A-01

Site Plan — “The Long Approach”

A 6-acre rectangular parcel (approx. 510′ × 512′). The house sits deep—280′ back from the road—behind a curving drive and a screen of trees, so you never see headlights from the house. Pool and outdoor living wrap the rear; tennis court tucks into the southeast corner with its own tree buffer.

SITE PLAN A 6.0 AC · 510′×512′ · SCALE 1″=80′
Residence + garage Pool / spa Tennis court Drive / motor court Lawn / meadow Woodland buffer
Why this works: The deep setback is the single biggest driver of the “estate” feeling, and it costs almost nothing—just driveway length (budget ~$18–25/linear ft for a quality paved drive, so a 600′ drive runs $11–15k). Keeping the pool and tennis court on the same side as the house clusters utilities and fencing, saving money versus scattering them across the parcel.
A-02

Site Plan — “The Courtyard Compound”

A squarer 7-acre parcel arranged around a central courtyard. The garage is detached and linked by a breezeway, creating a sheltered auto-court. This layout suits a flatter lot and gives a more contemporary, resort-like organisation—everything opens onto the pool courtyard.

SITE PLAN B 7.0 AC · 552′×552′ · SCALE 1″=85′
Residence + detached garage Courtyard pool Tennis court Auto court / drive Pasture / garden
Trade-off: A detached garage + breezeway costs roughly $40–70k more than an attached garage (separate foundation, roof, and the breezeway structure). You buy architectural drama and a sheltered courtyard for it. If budget is tight, Plan A’s attached garage is the saving.
A-03

Main Floor Plan

~2,950 sq ft. A single open volume runs kitchen–dining–great room across the rear, opening to the covered porch and pool. The primary suite sits on the main floor (increasingly the #1 request in the Southeast), with a secondary guest suite / office also down. Mudroom buffers the garage from the living space.

A-04

Upper Floor Plan

~1,850 sq ft. Three further bedrooms—one a full en-suite, two sharing a Jack-and-Jill—plus a generous bonus / media room over the garage and an open loft overlooking the foyer below. This is the “kids and guests” floor, fully separable from the main-floor primary suite.

UPPER FLOOR 1,850 SF · SCALE 1″=10′ · 3 BED + BONUS
Bonus / media Bedrooms Bath / wet Utility / flex Open loft
Flex move: The upper “Flex / Guest 6” room is the budget lever. Finish it as a 6th bedroom/second office if you want it, or leave it as unconditioned walk-in storage and bank ~$30–45k. Either way the framing and roof already exist, so adding it later is cheap.

The headline: Upstate SC lands closest to your $1.6M number while keeping the full program (pool + tennis + 5 beds). East Texas builds cheapest of all—but the ~$26k/yr property tax is the catch that, over 15 years, costs you ~$300k more than South Carolina. To pull any of these firmly under $1.6M: drop the tennis court (−$38k), finish 4 beds instead of 5 (−$45k), and trim the build to 4,400 sf (−$90k). That trio brings even the NC and TN options comfortably below budget.
