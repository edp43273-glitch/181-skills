~2,680 sq ft. Upper Floor Plan

~1,840 sq ft. A gallery loft runs along the great-room void (railed, overlooking the living space below). Three bedrooms—two with private balconies—plus a media/bonus room, a full hall bath, a Jack-and-Jill, an en suite, and a dedicated laundry. The garage roof below means no fourth bay of rooms, keeping the upstairs tight and efficient.
