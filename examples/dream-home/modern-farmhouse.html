The Modern Farmhouse

~5,100 sq ft across two storeys. All four bedrooms are upstairs — each with a private en suite and walk-in closet. The master suite commands the entire east wing of the upper floor with French doors onto a 100′ full-width rear balcony that runs the full breadth of the house above the covered patio. Ground floor is all social: open-plan great room under a 20′ double-height ceiling, kitchen with directly-connected walk-in pantry, study, two guest suites, and a 3-car garage. Stair, void, and structural grid are exactly aligned between sheets.
