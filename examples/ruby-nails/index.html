Born from a passion for perfection

Ruby Nails began in a small London studio with one idea: nail colour shouldn't compromise. Our founder spent three years perfecting a formula that lasts up to 14 days without chipping, looks salon-fresh, and nourishes while it wears.

Today, we're the UK's fastest-growing nail care brand — trusted by professional manicurists and home artists alike. Every shade is cruelty-free, vegan, and made in our certified British facility.

The Ritual

Four steps to perfection

Our signature application ritual gives you salon-quality results at home, every time.

01
✨

Prep & Cleanse

Shape, buff, and cleanse nails with our Prep Oil Remover to ensure a perfectly clean surface for maximum adhesion.

02
💅

Base Coat

Apply our fortifying Base Coat in one thin layer. It locks in nourishment and creates the perfect adhesion foundation.

03
🎨

Colour — Two Coats

Apply two thin layers of your chosen shade. Thin coats dry faster and last far longer than a single thick application.

04
💎

Top Coat Seal

Finish with our Crystal Top Coat for a mirror-glass shine and maximum chip protection for up to 14 days.
