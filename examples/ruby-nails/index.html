Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How long does Ruby Nails formula last? +
Our patented long-wear formula is engineered to last up to three weeks without chipping when applied correctly with a base and top coat.
What makes it 16-free? +
We’ve removed 16 commonly used toxic chemicals including formaldehyde, toluene, DBP, camphor, formaldehyde resin, xylene, ethyl tosylamide, parabens, and 8 more. Our formula is independently certified.
Is Ruby Nails vegan and cruelty-free? +
Yes — entirely. We are PETA-certified vegan and cruelty-free. No animal-derived ingredients, no animal testing, at any stage of production or supply chain.
What’s your return policy? +
We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you’re not completely satisfied, we’ll arrange a replacement or full refund, no questions asked.
How do I get the best results? +
Start with clean, oil-free nails. Apply our Ruby Base Coat, two thin coats of colour, and finish with our Glossy Top Coat. Reapply top coat every 3–4 days to extend wear.
