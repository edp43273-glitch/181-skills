The Method

The four-step ritual.

01
Cleanse & Prep

Begin with clean, dry nails. Remove any oils with our Prep Cleanser for maximum adhesion and a flawless finish.

02
Base Coat

Apply one thin coat of Crystal Clear Base. This creates a protective layer that prevents staining and extends wear.

03
Colour

Apply two thin coats of your chosen shade, allowing 60 seconds between coats for that flawless, streak-free finish.

04
Seal & Shine

Finish with Gold Dust Top Coat to lock in colour and add a mirror-like shine. Your nails, perfected for up to 14 days.
